FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is it really free for the hotel?

Yes. The review, the recommendation and the introduction cost your hotel nothing. We're paid by the provider only if you decide to work with them.

Which providers do you work with?

Established hospitality-tech companies in AI guest messaging, guest experience, spa and wellness, and property management. We name the provider that fits once we understand your needs, so the advice comes first.

Will this replace my team?

No. The goal is to take repetitive questions and copy-paste work off your team, so they have more time for guests who need a real person.

We already have a PMS. Do we need to change it?

Usually not. Most of the tools we recommend connect to the systems you already use. If something doesn't integrate well, we'll tell you before you spend any time on a demo.

What size of property do you work with?

Hotels, resorts and serviced apartments, typically from around 50 rooms or units. Villa and apartment operators count all the units they manage.

What if the timing isn't right?

That's completely fine. Just tell us, and we won't keep chasing you.

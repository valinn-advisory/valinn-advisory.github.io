Guest questions wait too long

About 70 messages a day arrive across email, WhatsApp, Instagram and OTA chat. Between 23:00 and 07:00 nobody answers. Our test question about a sea-view room got a reply after four hours, with a link to the website instead of an answer.

04Our recommendations

Small steps first. Measure the impact of each step before expanding.

Start with an AI assistant connected to the PMS, with hand-off to staff

It can reply within minutes, day and night, in the guest's language. Check this in the demo and the pilot, not only on paper. It answers from real data (rooms, prices, policies) and passes anything it can't confirm, like an early check-in, to the front desk. Run it as a 60-day pilot on WhatsApp and the website first.

First reply time% answered without staffDirect bookings from chat

Fix the re-typing before buying anything new

Check whether your PMS and channel manager already offer a certified two-way connection that passes special requests. Often it exists and just isn't switched on. This costs little and could save about two hours a day Estimate.

What we would not do now

  • Replace the PMS in the middle of the year. Plan a PMS review in 12 months instead.
  • Buy a separate spa system before checking whether the guest platform can cover spa booking.
  • Commit to a long contract before a new tool has proven its value. Check the exit terms first.

Our evaluation framework

How we evaluate providers

We use the same method for every hotel, so our advice doesn't depend on who pays the most.

What we check

  1. Proven in hotels like yours, by size, category and type, not only in demos.
  2. Real integrations: certified, two-way connection with your PMS and channels.
  3. Full cost: setup, monthly fee, per-message or per-booking fees, add-ons, contract length.
  4. Support in your language and time zone, and how fast they answer.
  5. Your data: GDPR, who owns the data, and whether you can export it if you leave.
  6. Easy start: a pilot on one task or one property before a big decision.

How we compare

  • Same test for everyone. We ask each option the same real guest questions and compare the answers.
  • Written price breakdown for your hotel size, not a price range from a website.
  • Hotel references: we look for hotels already using the tool and what they say about it.
  • Fit with your team: who will use it every day, and how much training it needs.

Risks we look for

  • Hidden costs that appear after signing: extra channels, languages, messages.
  • Long lock-in contracts with no exit if the tool doesn't work.
  • "Integration" that only works one way, so staff still re-type.
  • AI that promises without checking: it should confirm from real data or hand off to staff.
  • Data you can't take with you when you change provider.
